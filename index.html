Electrochemical interfaces and functional materials

My research connects electrochemistry, surface science and materials chemistry to understand how composition, structure and interfacial transformations govern activity, selectivity and stability.

About & contact

Pouya Hosseini

I am an electrochemist and materials scientist specializing in X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), electrochemical interfaces, electrocatalysis and surface-sensitive characterization of functional materials. I am a Research Scientist at the Max Planck Institute for Sustainable Materials in Düsseldorf. Independently, I develop interactive music-theory applications and compose and perform Persian music on setar and santur. My independent applications are the Persian Music Scales App, Urmawī Advar Atlas and World Music Scales Atlas.
