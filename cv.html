Research profile

Electrochemist and materials scientist with hands-on XPS experience since 2016, specializing in surface analysis, electrochemical interfaces, electrocatalysis, functional materials and surface-sensitive/in situ characterization. My work connects electrochemistry, surface science, and materials chemistry to understand structure–property–activity relationships in batteries, electrocatalysis, and electroactive thin-film systems. I combine advanced electrochemical methods with XPS, AFM, XRD, IR spectroscopy, ATR-SEIRAS, PM-IRRAS, and UV-Vis/NIR spectroelectrochemistry, with experience in research leadership, student mentoring, international collaboration, and industrial R&D.

  • Research Scientist at the Max Planck Institute for Sustainable Materials.
  • Previous role as Research Scientist / Group Leader at Ruhr-Universität Bochum, coordinating research on atomic-scale characterization, electrochemical interfaces, energy materials, and surface science.
  • Postdoctoral researcher at Max-Planck-Institut für Eisenforschung and Ruhr University Bochum, focusing on electrocatalyst nanomaterials, reaction-specific catalysts, solid/liquid interfaces, and spectroelectrochemical characterization.
  • Peer-reviewed publications, including first-author and corresponding-author contributions, with work published in leading journals in electrochemistry, materials chemistry, surface science, and catalysis.
  • Broad technical portfolio covering electrochemistry, surface-sensitive spectroscopy, structural characterization, thin-film analysis, and data interpretation.
  • Experience in supervising MSc and PhD students, coordinating collaborative projects, preparing reports and accepted synchrotron beamtime proposals, and communicating research in international environments.

X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) expertise

Since 2016, X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) has been central to my research and collaborative surface analysis. My experience spans instrument operation and maintenance, experimental design, quantitative analysis and the physically consistent interpretation of complex spectra.

  • Instrumentation and experimental design: Hands-on experience with Thermo Fisher Scientific, Scienta Omicron and SPECS platforms, including Thermo Avantage software, laboratory near-ambient-pressure XPS (NAP-XPS) and synchrotron hard X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (HAXPES). Sample suitability, acquisition planning, sample introduction, calibration, charge compensation, routine maintenance and vacuum troubleshooting.
  • Advanced spectral interpretation: Chemical-state analysis and quantification of complex transition-metal spectra, including multiplet splitting, shake-up and charge-transfer satellites, overlapping signals and coexisting oxidation states. Critical assessment of backgrounds, charging, energy referencing, contamination, sample heterogeneity and non-unique peak-fitting solutions.
  • Method development and reproducibility: Development of MATLAB-based workflows for systematic, reproducible spectral deconvolution and evaluation. I emphasize physically defensible interpretations consistent with reference data, material chemistry, experimental conditions and the limitations of XPS.
  • Materials and complementary techniques: Applications to electrocatalysts, heterogeneous and hydrothermal catalysts, transition-metal oxides, functional and conductive thin films, battery interfaces and solid-electrolyte interphases (SEI). I interpret XPS alongside electrochemistry, AFM, XRD, infrared spectroscopy and microscopy evidence.
  • Instrument responsibility and researcher training: At Ruhr University Bochum, I served as a principal XPS contact, responsible for routine system upkeep, operational checks and troubleshooting. I have trained MSc and PhD researchers in measurement planning, instrument operation, data processing, artefact recognition and responsible interpretation, and supported collaborative research and synchrotron beamtime proposals.

For research context and selected publications, see my XPS and surface analysis research profile.
