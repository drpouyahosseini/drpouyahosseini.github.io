Research profile

Electrochemist and materials scientist specializing in electrochemical interfaces, electrocatalysis, functional materials, and surface-sensitive/in situ characterization. My work connects electrochemistry, surface science, and materials chemistry to understand structure–property–activity relationships in batteries, electrocatalysis, and electroactive thin-film systems. I combine advanced electrochemical methods with XPS, AFM, XRD, IR spectroscopy, ATR-SEIRAS, PM-IRRAS, and UV-Vis/NIR spectroelectrochemistry, with experience in research leadership, student mentoring, international collaboration, and industrial R&D.

  • Research Scientist / Group Leader at Ruhr-Universität Bochum, coordinating research on atomic-scale characterization, electrochemical interfaces, energy materials, and surface science.
  • Postdoctoral researcher at Max-Planck-Institut für Eisenforschung and Ruhr University Bochum, focusing on electrocatalyst nanomaterials, reaction-specific catalysts, solid/liquid interfaces, and spectroelectrochemical characterization.
  • 25+ peer-reviewed publications, including first-author and corresponding-author contributions, with work published in leading journals in electrochemistry, materials chemistry, surface science, and catalysis.
  • Broad technical portfolio covering electrochemistry, surface-sensitive spectroscopy, structural characterization, thin-film analysis, and data interpretation.
  • Experience in supervising MSc and PhD students, coordinating collaborative projects, preparing reports and accepted synchrotron beamtime proposals, and communicating research in international environments.
