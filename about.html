Pouya Hosseini

I am an electrochemist and materials scientist specializing in electrochemical interfaces, electrocatalysis and surface-sensitive characterization of functional materials. My Research Scientist appointment at the Max Planck Institute for Sustainable Materials in Düsseldorf is effective 1 December 2026. Independently, I develop interactive music-theory applications and compose and perform Persian music on setar and santur. My independent applications are the Persian Music Scales App, Urmawī Advar Atlas and World Music Scales Atlas.
